# WP-1.1f Worker, Lease, Recovery, Idempotency Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Worker Celery yang aman, lease satu-eksekusi per task, recovery sweep, dan `Idempotency-Key` sesuai API_CONTRACTS §1.

**Architecture:**
- Lease dan recovery adalah fungsi `workflow/` yang memakai `transition` dan `commit_and_publish`.
- Celery hanya pembungkus tipis: `jobs` memanggil `asyncio.run(...)`.
- Idempotency adalah middleware ASGI di dalam CSRF, dengan penyimpanan di Redis.

**Tech Stack:** Celery 5.6 (Redis broker), SQLAlchemy async, redis.asyncio, pytest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-wp-1.1f-worker-lease-recovery-idempotency-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- **Lease:** `LEASE_SECONDS = 60`, `HEARTBEAT_SECONDS = 20`. Batas `MAX_ACTIVE_TASKS` berasal dari `Settings.max_active_tasks`.
- **Recovery:** aktor `Actor("system", "recovery")`. Reason `lease_expired` atau `cancel_requested`.
- **Idempotency:**
  - key Redis `kk:idem:<sha256>`, TTL 86400;
  - format kunci `^[A-Za-z0-9_.:-]{1,128}$`;
  - batas body yang disimpan 1 MiB;
  - header replay `Idempotent-Replay: true`.
- **Celery:** hanya JSON, `acks_late`, `reject_on_worker_lost`, prefetch 1, antrean default `ide`, beat `recovery-sweep` 30 dtk.
- **Layer:** `app.worker` setara `app.main` (`"app.main | app.worker"`).

## Review Focus
1. **Dua claim paralel di task yang sama** → tepat satu yang mendapat lease. Test: `test_parallel_claims_one_winner`.
2. **Claim paralel di task berbeda saat batas `MAX_ACTIVE` = 1** → tepat satu sukses. Test: `test_max_active_holds_under_parallel_claims`.
3. **Dua sweep bersamaan** → setiap task hanya punya satu transisi dan satu event. Test: `test_parallel_sweeps_transition_once`.
4. **Idempotency-Key yang sama dipakai sesi lain** → tidak di-replay. Test: `test_other_session_is_not_replayed`.
5. **Respons pertama memasang cookie** → replay tidak membawa `Set-Cookie`. Test: `test_replay_never_sets_cookies`.
